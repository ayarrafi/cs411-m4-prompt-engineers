Gradient Descent Lab

A 30-minute lesson on gradient descent in one dimension: explore freely, learn the update rule, solve missions, then try a new example on your own.

Support condition
Learning goals
  1. Explain and apply the 1D gradient descent update rule, and relate the gradient to the direction of an update.
  2. Predict how changing the learning rate or the starting point affects the optimization trajectory.
  3. Diagnose why gradient descent reaches a global or local minimum, converges slowly, oscillates, or diverges.
